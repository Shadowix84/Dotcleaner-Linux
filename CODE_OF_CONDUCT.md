# Code of conduct

Dotcleaner is made by retro gaming fans, for retro gaming fans, in five languages. Everyone is welcome.

**Be kind and respectful.** Help newcomers, assume good intentions, and keep discussions about the
project. Write in English or in your own language — someone will help translate.

**Not accepted:** insults, harassment, discrimination of any kind, spam, or sharing ROMs and other
copyrighted files. Issues are for the tool, not for finding games.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and block people who do not follow
these rules. To report a problem privately, use the contact in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
